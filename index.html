What this does not tell you

No timing. A lane that covers five paths but that you reach too late looks exactly as good here as one you own from the first second.

Bunker dimensions. Every height and width used here is unverified: Beam, Brick, Can / Cylinder, Large Dorito, Mini, Pin, Small Dorito, Snake, Tall, Temple. Heights decide what blocks a shot, so a wrong height flips a sightline.

Two and a half D. A shot is either over a bunker or stopped by it. Leaning, kneeling between the two stances and shooting through gaps are not modelled.

About this layout. NOT a real tournament layout. Built by hand on the 10 ft grid so features 04 to 07 have something to run on until the real layout is captured with tools/capture.html into data/layout.json.
